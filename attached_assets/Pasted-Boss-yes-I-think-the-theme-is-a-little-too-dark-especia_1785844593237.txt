Boss 😂 — yes, I think the theme is a little too dark, especially on mobile.
The design itself is excellent: the neon indigo/green/orange accents, monospace headings, telemetry aesthetic, and glass panels give it a convincing mission-control / autonomous AI platform identity. But the current background-to-surface contrast is compressed too much.
What I see in the screenshots
The main problem is that several layers are very close to black:
page background → almost black
cards → almost black
card inner surfaces → almost black
inactive text → very dark gray
borders → extremely subtle
So when you scroll through the dashboard, large portions visually merge together.
For example, the Mission Control card has a beautiful structure, but the right-side panel and surrounding background are difficult to distinguish. The Overseer page has the same issue: the cards are visually sophisticated, but their boundaries disappear unless you look carefully.
I would NOT make it dramatically lighter
Don't turn it into a normal #111827 / gray dashboard. 😅
Your current aesthetic is valuable.
I'd move it from:
"black sci-fi console"
toward:
"deep-space professional control center."
Something approximately like:
CURRENT
Page:       near #05060A
Surface:    near #08090F
Cards:      near #090A10
Borders:    extremely subtle

TARGET

Surface:    #0D111B
Cards:      #101522

The exact values aren't important; the hierarchy is.

One thing you did REALLY well
Don't remove the colored status system.
Your:
🟢 green → healthy/live
🟡 amber → degraded/warning
🔴 red → failure
🟣 indigo/violet → system intelligence / active UI
is excellent.
In fact, I'd make the neutral surfaces slightly brighter while keeping the status colors exactly as strong.
That will make the status signals stand out even more.
Mobile specifically
The first screenshot demonstrates why this matters.
You have:
MISSION CONTROL
SYSTEM DEGRADED
3 SERVICES ONLINE
0 ACTIVE WORKFLOWS
589ms
ACTIVE MISSION
100%
1 WARNING
That's a lot of information.
On a small screen, the user needs stronger visual grouping. The current darkness makes the interface feel slightly muddy.
I'd target roughly:
Background: 8/10 dark
Cards: 6.5/10 dark
Elevated cards: 5.5/10 dark
Borders: 3/10 visible
Primary text: 9/10
Secondary text: 6/10
Telemetry/status colors: 10/10
That would preserve the premium aesthetic while dramatically improving readability.
One more thing I'd change
Your "1 WARNING" floating pill is visually competing with the content.
In both screenshots it sits over important cards. It looks cool, but functionally it is slightly intrusive.
I'd make it a persistent system-status rail/toast that docks near the bottom but doesn't overlap primary content, or reserve a small status area above the bottom navigation.
My verdict 😎
Don't redesign it. Refine the contrast.
The visual language is already strong enough to become the signature identity of Mutex/Kanyoza Overseer.
I'd make only a ~10–20% luminance increase in the neutral surfaces, strengthen card separation, improve secondary-text contrast, and leave the neon status colors alone.
That would take it from:
🔥 "Cool hacker dashboard"
to:
🔥🔥 "Professional autonomous operations console."
And honestly, the Overseer screen is already very close. The architecture of the visual hierarchy is right; it's mainly the neutral contrast that needs tuning.
